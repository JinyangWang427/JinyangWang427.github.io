Autonomous Driving Algorithm Intern · Changan Auto 2026.01 — 2026.07

Multi-View World Models · RGB-LiDAR Streaming Generation

DiTCross-View AttentionRGB-LiDARDistributed Training
  • Contributed to a pose-controllable multi-modal driving world model based on Wan2.1 DiT, extending the six-camera data organization, unified latent layout, and multi-view generation pipeline; designed ring-style local cross-view attention that reduced allowed cross-view Q-K connections by 50%.
